The Orbital transfer mission teaches orbital energy changes, circularization at apogee and impulse budget management. The learner moves a fictional vehicle from 400 to 900 km without impact. All conditions and references are synthetic.

The library extends competency tracking to six families. Advanced docking, recovery and deorbiting use measured conditions from the shared nonlinear inertial engine. Inspection and separation retain their disclosed linear educational executor. Authoring customizes three objective labels and scenario parameters while retaining the selected family's verifiable rubric. Assessment uses no generative models. A score is evidence of implemented checks in a synthetic exercise, not a validated measure of real operator competence.

ORB01 maps first-burn planning to the competency Interpret the orbital transfer. Evidence requires a prograde first burn within 10 meters per second of the analytic reference. ORB02 maps orbit acquisition to Circularize at apogee. Both apsides must lie within 15 km and eccentricity must not exceed 0.003. ORB03 maps resource preservation to Manage propellant. Evidence requires acquired orbit, at most three burns and total impulse no more than 20 meters per second above reference.

Scaffolding begins with Demonstration, which executes the three reference events with pauses. Guided mode exposes a contextual instruction, preview and burn calculation. Practice removes the instruction and calculation; telemetry and prediction remain operational tools. Assessment shows evidence and a recommendation. Demonstration provides formative evidence only and must not certify individual competency.

Immediate feedback shows updated apsides, mass loss and competency state after each command. Delayed feedback provides final error, total impulse, propellant, maneuver count and event replay. Score is the fraction of three achieved objectives rounded to a hundred. Completion requires ORB02; full score requires all three objectives.

Full transfer achievement proposes Precision practice with a 3 km tolerance. Incomplete performance retains the configured tolerance and recommends revisiting the missing stage. This adaptation belongs to the legacy transfer executor. Advanced mission guidance and recommendations respond to measured current conditions, but advanced missions do not automatically rewrite difficulty, capture tolerances or client scenarios during a run. Longitudinal learner profiling and adaptive curriculum selection are not implemented.

| Deorbiting | ADV2 | Establish an entry intersecting orbit | Computed osculating perigee is below 120 km. The selected lower perigee remains the maneuver target. |
| Deorbiting | ADV3 | Verify descending interface arrival | The localized 120 km event has radial velocity at least the configured minimum magnitude in the descending direction. |

An advanced score is the rounded fraction of the three achieved checks. Completion additionally requires every check, no collision, no abort and expenditure within budget. Capture and successful completion are distinct: a docking capture after a corridor deviation can terminate the attempt with an incomplete rubric. A failed synchronization command must not award recovery alignment. The target attitude model is a bounded scalar teaching model, not evidence of a physical capture mechanism.

For advanced missions, an instructor first demonstrates the initial state, numerical limits and ordered procedure. Guided practice exposes hold commands and measured completion criteria. Independent practice uses manual RTN impulses and propagation within the same budget. Verification uses explicit capture or measured entry, followed by the report and journal. A learner can inspect historical commands without changing the authoritative live state. Demonstration or guided success must not be represented as an independently assessed human skill.

Immediate feedback includes current separation, speed, corridor, roll, resource expenditure and rejected commands. Delayed feedback includes achieved objectives, deviations, hold acquisition evidence, entry conditions, recommendations and replay. Before a supervised assessment, the instructor fixes the mission revision, permitted assistance, numerical criteria and interpretation of each objective label. The retained snapshot keeps later edits from changing the meaning of the result.

Institutional recognition requires a validated rubric, trained supervision, learning outcome studies and any required LMS integration. No learning gain, transfer of training or certification outcome has been measured by the implementation tests.
