Customer evaluation protocol

Purpose

Determine whether Orbital helps a training team prepare, execute and review one of its own bounded orbital exercises. This protocol measures a workflow rather than inferring usefulness from the number of features.

Scope agreement

Use one docking, recovery or deorbit scenario. Define the intended learner, instructor, installation environment and acceptable physics fidelity. Use synthetic or explicitly approved data. Record whether the target is cooperative, how relative attitude is represented and which criteria constitute completion. Keep the scalar attitude and atmospheric interface limits visible in the agreement.

Baseline

Ask the instructor to prepare an equivalent exercise using the current process. Observe preparation time, corrections, separate tools used and the information retained after execution. Do not change the scoring rubric midway through the comparison. If the tasks differ materially, report that difference rather than calculating a misleading percentage improvement.

Orbital session

Create the customer's collection and copy the selected template. Change at least one initial condition, one vehicle property and one acceptance threshold. Save the revision. Execute the exercise with the instructor observing. Retain an incomplete or deliberately rejected attempt as well as a completed run. Confirm that the outcome responds to the edited conditions.

Review

Have the instructor reconstruct the relevant decisions from the after action report and replay without consulting the developer. Record missing information, incorrect interpretation and time spent. Restore the archive and compare the state and report with the original. Export the selected collection and inspect it for unrelated records.

Acceptance record

The customer should decide whether the exercise covers its instructional task, whether model limits are acceptable, whether the final evidence supports discussion, and whether delivery fits the intended environment. A failed criterion should produce an issue with a reproducible archive and an agreed retest. Do not substitute a polished screenshot for a missing acceptance result.

Commercial decision

Only after this evaluation should the parties define a paid scope such as scenario authoring, internal deployment, integration or maintained content. Record support boundaries, permitted use, required validation, ownership of customer scenarios and change control. No price, service level or delivery date is implied by this protocol.

Publication permission

Customer identity, results and statements remain private unless the customer separately permits publication. An internal positive evaluation is not a public endorsement. This protocol provides no such permission.
