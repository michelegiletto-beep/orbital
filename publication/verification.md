Verification boundaries

Exact replay was established in the documented runtime, not as a guarantee of bit identical floating point results across every platform. A passing exercise is an implemented objective check, not a validated measure of operator competence. No independent physical validation, measured learning benefit, customer acceptance or quantified economic benefit has been established.

Docker, physical headsets, smart cards, an institutional identity provider, external FIPS validated key custody, immutable external audit storage, real multiplayer load and government accreditation were not available for this verification. The deployment definitions require recipient environment validation. Scalar attitude, logical capture, engineering drag and termination at the atmospheric interface remain disclosed model limits.
